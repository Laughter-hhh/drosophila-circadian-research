## Paper and scope

Roessingh, S., Rosing, M., Marunova, M. et al. Temperature synchronization of the *Drosophila* circadian clock protein PERIOD is controlled by the TRPA channel PYREXIA. *Commun. Biol.* **2**, 246 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-019-0497-0. Version of record published 1 July 2019; open access. Article: https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-019-0497-0

### Bilingual article map

**中文：** 这篇文章研究环境温度周期怎样同步果蝇生物钟。作者发现，TRPA 通道 PYREXIA (PYX) 缺失会破坏对 20 °C:16 °C 低幅温度周期的行为同步；切除触角可部分改善突变体同步，提示异常信号可能从触角/外周神经系统传入。突变还伴随 pyx 表达细胞 TRIC 报告信号升高，以及 s-LNv 与 DN1 中 PER 表达相位或阳性细胞数改变。文章没有在 s-LNv 中直接记录膜电位/放电，也没有直接证明 PYX 在 16–25 °C 下被温度直接门控或识别了具体的突触路径。

**English:** The paper asks how environmental temperature cycles synchronize the fly clock. Loss of the TRPA channel PYREXIA (PYX) disrupts behavioral synchronization to a shallow 20 °C:16 °C cycle; antennal removal partially improves the mutant phenotype, implicating an abnormal peripheral signal. Mutants also show higher TRIC reporter output in pyx-expressing cells and altered PER expression timing/counts in s-LNv and DN1. The study does not directly record s-LNv voltage/firing, demonstrate direct PYX gating at 16–25 °C, or map the synaptic route.

**Research gap / 研究缺口.** Prior work had linked PYX to temperature synchronization, but the intervening sensory tissue and central molecular-clock consequences were unresolved. This paper tests the antenna/peripheral nervous system and central PER readouts. It is an inference from the paper sequence that this gap motivated the study; the authors’ private motives are not established.

## Recall questions / 自测题

1. **What is the paper’s direct behavioral result? / 直接行为结果是什么？** pyx³ impairs synchronization to the tested cool temperature cycle; antenna removal partially improves it.
2. **Does Fig. 3 directly measure Ca²⁺ with a microscope? / Fig. 3 是显微镜直接测钙吗？** No. TRIC drives luciferase transcription; it is an indirect, temporally filtered reporter.
3. **What does NaChBac establish? / NaChBac 结果说明什么？** Increasing excitability in pyx-GAL4 cells can mimic key behavior, but does not prove direct PYX gating or the synaptic route.
4. **What are the main clock-neuron PER readouts? / 主要时钟神经元 PER 结果是什么？** s-LNv PER timing shifts and DN1 PER-positive counts fall during parts of the cycle; dorsal 8.0-luc reporter rhythms respond poorly to a temperature-cycle delay.
5. **What is the main unsupported link? / 最主要未证实的因果环节是什么？** The exact peripheral PYX cell and synapse that alter native s-LNv/DN1 electrical activity.

## Conclusions, evidence labels, and limits

- **Direct observations｜直接观察：** Under the tested shallow temperature cycles, pyx³ mutants show abnormal behavior; antennal ablation partly improves phase alignment; pyx³ raises the TRIC reporter under temperature cycling; constitutive NaChBac activation in pyx-GAL4 cells mimics key behavioral features; PER readouts change in s-LNv/DN1 and dorsal reporter populations.
- **Inference｜推断：** PYX may normally gate or restrain temperature-related sensory signals reaching central clock cells. The proposed antennal-pyx-to-anterior-cell-to-clock-neuron route is plausible but not directly traced in this paper.
- **Not established｜未建立：** Direct PYX current or gating at 16–25 °C; native s-LNv membrane-potential or firing changes; a causal chain from altered s-LNv PER to behavior; the identity of every peripheral PYX cell responsible; independent fly counts behind each hemisphere-based immunostaining n.
